# Tailor

> Tailor is a customizable, headless ERP platform built for modern retail operators. It unifies inventory, orders, purchasing, production, and accounting into one composable, API-first system that integrates with tools like Shopify, QuickBooks, and Odoo — typically going live in weeks rather than the 12–18 months required by traditional monolithic ERPs.

Tailor serves mid-market and enterprise retail, e-commerce, and manufacturing brands. The platform combines a headless architecture (granular API services and custom UIs), modular ERP components (activate only what you need), and AI-powered inventory intelligence. Customers include omnichannel retailers, B2B + DTC hybrid brands, and manufacturers replacing legacy systems such as SAP and NetSuite.
